# NEST ESCAPE — Claw Chaos playtest, round 2

## Goal
Find out whether the new claw timing challenge is clear, fair, and fun before adding Room 02.

## Who to ask
Two people who have not watched the game being built. Ideally, one plays on a phone and one on a laptop/desktop.

## Set-up
- Give them the game file/package.
- Say only: **“Try to escape the arcade room. Tell me what you think when you are done.”**
- Do not explain the controls, claw, jump, or intended route unless they ask.
- Let each tester play for up to three minutes.

## Watch for
1. Do they notice the EXIT straight away?
2. Do they understand that the claw is dangerous?
3. Do they wait for its swing, or try to run straight through?
4. Is the first loss understandable?
5. Do they discover jumping naturally?
6. Can they finish at least once?
7. Do phone controls feel usable?

## Ask after they play
Copy their answers as closely as possible:

1. What was the most fun moment?
2. What confused you, if anything?
3. Did the claw feel fair? Why or why not?
4. Did you understand what to do without help?
5. Would you try Room 02?
6. What finish time and CLAWED count did you get?

## What counts as success
- They can identify the EXIT and the claw’s danger without being told.
- At least one run is completed within three minutes.
- A loss feels like a mistimed dash, not a mystery.
- They can name one enjoyable moment.

## Decision after two testers
Change only the biggest repeated problem. If timing is clear and fair, preserve the room and start planning Room 02.
