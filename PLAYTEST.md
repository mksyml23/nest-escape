# NEST ESCAPE — Claw Chaos playtest

## For the player
1. Play without being told how to win.
2. Try to reach the neon EXIT.
3. Play for about three minutes.
4. At the end, say:
   - One thing that was fun
   - One thing that confused you
   - Whether you would play a second room

## For Maksym
Do not explain the controls unless the player asks. Watch for:
- Do they notice the exit?
- Do they understand the claw is dangerous?
- Do they discover jump naturally?
- Do they get stuck, or does a loss feel unfair?

## What to record
- Finish time and number of claw hits shown in-game
- Exact point of confusion, if any
- The single biggest improvement to make next

The test is successful if the player can complete one run, knows why they lost, and can name one enjoyable moment.
